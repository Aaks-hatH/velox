Before you use Velox Health

Velox Health is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, prevent, or predict any illness or condition. It shows statistical comparisons between your recent measurements and your own history, nothing more.

It can't replace your doctor. Only a qualified healthcare professional can examine you, understand your full medical history, and tell you what your health data means.

Don't depend on this app alone. Use it as one source of information alongside regular check-ups and professional advice, never instead of them.

It can be wrong or miss things. Watch sensors record only some of the time and can be affected by fit, movement, and temperature. Days can be missing. No alert never means everything is fine, and an alert doesn't mean something is wrong.

Suggestions and alerts are general. Recommendations, alerts and warnings use your profile and your own history, but they are general information, not medical advice made for you.

Don't change any treatment because of it. Never start, stop, or change a medicine, diet, or treatment based on Velox Health. Talk to your doctor or pharmacist first.

Don't wait because of it. If you feel unwell or something worries you, contact a healthcare professional, even if Velox Health shows nothing unusual.

Assistant answers have limits. The assistant, including the optional cloud AI, can make mistakes. Its answers are general information, not medical advice.

In an emergency, such as chest pain, trouble breathing, or fainting, call your local emergency number straight away. Don't use Velox Health.
